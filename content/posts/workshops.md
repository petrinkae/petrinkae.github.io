Past workshops have included:
   - Introductions to solutions journalism
   - Data journalism storytelling
   - Intermediate to advanced Excel
   - Intro to SQL for storytelling
   - Critical data exploration with spreadsheets
   - Data cleaning
   - Building national stories from local datasets
   - Working with bad and limited data
   - In-depth investigations of property developers and landlords
   - Covering transgender communities on your beat
   - Transgender 101 for workplaces

I'm an experienced moderator, and I love speaking with students.
