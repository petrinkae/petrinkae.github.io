# Speaking

I'm a frequent moderator, panelist, public speaker, and interactive workshop leader.

I develop workshops for both youth and adult learners. Most often, I present on data, speaking on specific tools like spreadsheets or thinking through complex problems like working with incomplete data. I've also led public media trainings on telling your own story. 

Past partners have included the Open Visualization Academy, Education Writers Association, and the Reynolds Journalism Institute, as well as multiple investigative journalism associations. I'm also a contributor and recurring teacher for [First Python Notebook](https://palewi.re/docs/first-python-notebook/concat.html).
